Pando

Pando is a giant clonal colony of a single male quaking aspen (Populus tremuloides)
tree located in Utah's Fishlake National Forest. It is considered one of the oldest and largest
living organisms on Earth, covering over 106 acres and weighing an estimated 6,000 tons.

Biologist all over the world travel to see Pando to study its unique characteristics and genetic makeup.
Pando's age is estimated to be around 80,000 years old, making it one of the oldest living organisms on Earth.
Pando's root system is interconnected, allowing it to share nutrients and water through its stems.
Pando's ability to regenerate itself through cloning has allowed it to survive for thousands of years, even in the face of environmental challenges.
Its size and longevity make it a fascinating subject of study for scientists interested in ecology, genetics, and the resilience of life on Earth.
It's impressive age can give us insights of the last ice age and how climate change has impacted the region over millennia.
