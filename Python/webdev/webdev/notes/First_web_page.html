Ruy Lopez opening

The world's best opening

The Ruy Lopez opening is one of the most popular openings in chess.
It is one of the most used among grandmasters of chess due to its
agressive tactics and traps that anyone can fall into.It is played

  1. e4, e5
  2. Nf3, Nc6
  3. Bb5, a6
  4. Ba4, Nf6
  5. O-O, Be7

known as the Ruy Lopez closed position. Top players including world renound
Magnus Carlsen often cite the Ruy Lopez as their favorite opening noting that
it's highly reliable and strategic.


Learning Journey

The Ruy Lopez opening is cited as one of the hardest and most tedious opeings to learn
due to its complexity and extensive theory. The number of highly positioned variations
in the first couple moves reaches above 182, making it difficult to sustain a lead
in a long-term game. Studing the Ruy Lopez opening is the hardest thing a chess player can do,
the time and dedication needed to learn the theory is substantial.
